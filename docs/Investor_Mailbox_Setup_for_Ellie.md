# Connecting your mailbox to the Averroes deal tool

Hi Ellie,

Our deal tool will send investor introduction emails from your address (egabr@averroescapital.com), signed by you, and it will notice when someone replies. To do that it needs a special one-off password from Google called an "App Password". This is not your normal password, and it only works for this one purpose. Creating it takes about five minutes. Please follow the steps below exactly.

## Step 1: Make sure 2-Step Verification is switched on

Google only lets you create an App Password if 2-Step Verification is on.

1. Open a browser and go to https://myaccount.google.com
2. Make sure you are signed in as egabr@averroescapital.com (check the round picture or initial in the top right).
3. On the left, click **Security**.
4. Scroll to the section **How you sign in to Google** and look at **2-Step Verification**.
   - If it says **On**, go to Step 2.
   - If it says **Off**, click it, then click **Get started** and follow the prompts. Google will ask for your phone number and send you a code. Once it says **On**, come back here.

## Step 2: Create the App Password

1. Go to https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords
   (if Google asks you to sign in again, that is normal)
2. You will see a box that says **App name**. Type: **Averroes Deal Tool**
3. Click **Create**.
4. A yellow box appears with a 16-letter password, shown in four groups like this:
   `abcd efgh ijkl mnop`
5. Copy that whole 16-letter code straight away. Google shows it only once. If you lose it, you simply repeat Step 2 and make a new one.

## Step 3: Send it to Ishu

Send these three things to Ishu directly (a WhatsApp message or a phone call is fine; please do not forward it to anyone else):

1. The 16-letter App Password from Step 2
2. Confirm your title exactly as it should appear in the signature: **Director of Investor Relations**
3. The phone number you want in the email signature, if any (optional)

That is all. Ishu will plug it in and send you a test email so you can see exactly what investors will receive.

## What happens afterwards

- Emails the tool sends will appear in your **Sent** folder like any other email you send.
- When an investor replies, the reply lands in your normal inbox. The tool reads your inbox to notice the reply and update the investor's status. It does not delete or move anything.
- You can revoke the App Password at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords by clicking the bin icon next to "Averroes Deal Tool". The tool then stops sending immediately.

## If something does not work

- **"The setting you are looking for is not available for your account"** at Step 2: 2-Step Verification is not on yet. Go back to Step 1.
- **No App passwords page at all**: tell Ishu; the Google Workspace admin needs to allow it (Admin console → Security → Authentication → 2-Step Verification must be allowed for your account).
- **You made a password by mistake or lost it**: just make another one. Old ones can be deleted from the same page.
